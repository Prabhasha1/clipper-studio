Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Summary

Reel Studio runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read your channel name (youtube.readonly), so the app can show which channel is connected.
  • TikTok: your display name (user.info.basic) and permission to post videos (video.publish).
  • Instagram: your username and permission to publish Reels to your professional account.

The app never sees or stores your passwords. You sign in on each platform's own page.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens, app keys and your videos are saved only on your own computer, in the app's folder. They are never sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to, and only when you post a video.

What is sent to the platforms

When you click "Post now", the app sends that video, its title, its caption and your chosen privacy setting to the platform you picked. Nothing else is uploaded.

Deleting your data

Click "Disconnect" in the app's Accounts window to delete the saved tokens for that platform. You can also remove the app's access in your Google, TikTok or Instagram account settings. Deleting the app's folder removes everything it stored.

Children

The app isn't meant for children under 13.
