Reel Studio

A desktop app that makes short vertical edits (montages cut to music, dialogue scenes with subtitles, and lyric edits) and posts them to my own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  1. Reel Studio runs on my own computer. There is no online service or user sign-up.
  2. I choose a film or series, a style and a song. The app cuts the shots into short edits, adds captions or lyrics, and credits the music and footage.
  3. To post, I connect each account with the platform's official sign-in page (OAuth). The app never sees or stores my password.
  4. On each finished reel I click a platform button, review the caption, choose who can see it and press "Post now". Nothing is ever posted without that click.

Platforms

Posting uses only the official APIs: the YouTube Data API, the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram API. Reel Studio isn't made by, endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube, TikTok or Instagram.
