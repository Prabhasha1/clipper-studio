1. What Clipper Studio is

Clipper Studio ("the app") is a desktop application that runs on the user's own computer. It cuts videos into short clips and, when the user asks it to, posts those clips to the user's own YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account through each platform's official API.

2. Your content

You are responsible for everything you post with the app, including captions, and for following the rules of each platform: the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, and the Instagram Terms of Use.

3. Your accounts

You connect your accounts with each platform's own sign-in page. You can disconnect them at any time in the app, or remove the app's access from your account settings on each platform.

4. Posting

The app only posts a video when you click "Post now" on it. It doesn't post, like, follow or comment automatically.
